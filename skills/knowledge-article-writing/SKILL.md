# Knowledge article writing

A good article answers one question so well that nobody has to ask again.

## When

- You answered the same question two or three times.
- A knowledge gap was recorded (`record_knowledge_gap`) and you now know the answer.
- An existing article is wrong or out of date.

## Steps

1. Search first (`search_knowledge`). Improve an existing article rather than writing a second one.
2. Collect the facts from where they really are: solved tickets, the product, the owner. Never guess.
3. Write the draft:
   - **Title:** the question as a customer would ask it ("How do I connect my own domain?").
   - **First sentence:** the answer.
   - **Steps:** numbered, one action each, with the exact names of buttons and pages.
   - **If it doesn't work:** the two or three most common problems and their fixes.
   - **Related:** links to the next articles a reader needs.
4. Propose the draft (`propose_article_draft`). A person reviews and publishes it.

## Style

- Plain words, short sentences, no internal jargon.
- One article, one question.
- No prices, dates or promises unless the owner confirmed them for the article.
- No customer names or personal data in examples.
